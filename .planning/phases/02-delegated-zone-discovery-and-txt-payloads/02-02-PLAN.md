<objective>
Normalize selected-zone-relative TXT record owner computation for add and remove
while preserving the current OCI PATCH payload shape, TTL behavior, and
upstream-friendly code style.

Purpose: Fix delegated-subdomain and wildcard TXT payload behavior without
turning Phase 2 into a broad payload-builder or naming refactor.
Output: Shared selected-zone/owner computation in `dnsapi/dns_oci.sh` plus
mocked symmetry, wildcard, and escaping proof in `test/dns_oci_mock.sh`.
</objective>

<threat_model>
T-02-04: Add and remove could compute different owner names and leave stale TXT
records.
Mitigation: Route both public functions through one selected-zone/owner
computation and assert add/remove symmetry through captured PATCH bodies.

T-02-05: Changing the OCI payload `domain` field could break currently working
OCI DNS behavior.
Mitigation: Preserve the current fully qualified payload shape unless a failing
test proves it wrong.

T-02-06: Escaping fixes could balloon into a repo-wide JSON refactor.
Mitigation: Keep escaping local to OCI payload construction and cover only
realistic TXT/domain cases in focused tests.
</threat_model>
