<objective>
Tighten OCI zone discovery fallback behavior and expose enough mocked lookup
signal to prove ambiguous misses, visible authorization failures, factual debug,
and no-PATCH safety.

Purpose: Make delegated zone discovery reliable for headless automation without
changing OCI auth-mode selection or broadly refactoring request signing.
Output: Extended lookup fixtures in `test/dns_oci_mock.sh` and a minimal
`dnsapi/dns_oci.sh` fallback/error handling update.
</objective>

<threat_model>
T-02-01: Zone lookup fallback could hide a real authorization failure and mutate
a parent zone unexpectedly.
Mitigation: Hard-fail only when a visible authorization or permission signal is
available, and add a mocked hard-fail case that proves no PATCH occurs.

T-02-02: Lookup plumbing could accidentally change API-key authentication or
pull resource-principal behavior into Phase 2.
Mitigation: Keep `_oci_config` auth selection untouched and limit changes to
lookup status/error capture needed by `_get_zone`.

T-02-03: Debug output could leak sensitive material or over-interpret OCI 404s.
Mitigation: Debug only stable candidate/status/fallback facts, avoid auth
headers/tokens/private keys, and do not assert OCI-owned error prose.
</threat_model>
