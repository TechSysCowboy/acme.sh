<objective>
Harden the resource-principal signer around refresh, persistence, logging, and
terminal failure behavior.

Purpose: Ensure the new signer works through the public add path without
leaking or persisting ephemeral material, and that auth failures do not masquerade
as DNS zone lookup misses.
Output: Public-path mock tests for per-request refresh, no persistence/normal
logging, and terminal RP signing failures.
</objective>

<threat_model>
T-04-07: RPST/private PEM files could rotate between zone lookup and record
mutation, but the hook could reuse stale material.
Mitigation: Change file contents between mocked GET and PATCH and assert PATCH
uses the later token and key.

T-04-08: RP material could be saved, cleared, or normal-logged through inherited
config/debug paths.
Mitigation: Public-path tests assert account-config captures and normal logs
contain no RP secret values, paths, `ST$` keyId, signatures, or Authorization.

T-04-09: RP signing failure during zone lookup could continue to parent labels
and show misleading zone-not-found guidance.
Mitigation: Add a private terminal auth flag and assert parent lookup is not
attempted after RP signing failure.
</threat_model>
